# Basketball Simulator

An in-depth, stat-based simulator aiming to authentically replicate the game of basketball. Start your managerial career and lead your team to the championship!

## Features

* Make trades with other teams to bolster their team
* Negotiate contracts with their players and sign free agents to improve the team
* Adjust their gameplan to increase chances of winning
* Compete in the post-season for the ultimate prize - the championship - should they make it thus far
* Build for the future with draft picks which may also be traded
* Advanced stat generation algorithm based on rating, age, physical attributes (e.g. height), position and play style
* Advanced match engine incorporating player stats, team gameplan and overall team fit
* In-depth data analysis of each player showing their strengths and weaknesses among other data
* Data analysis for each team, allowing the player to compare their team abilities to the rest of the league
* Contract negotiation system ensuring a player's value matches their salary
* AI-between-AI and user-between-AI interactions via trades
